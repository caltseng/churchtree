Main Branch

Church in China

Selected episodes, 635-2024. Parallel display lanes are not institutional lifespans or a national church genealogy.

Last verified event: 2024-10-22. Gaps are not extinction dates.

Scope, gaps and evidence

Mainland historical settings, with Macau as a mission hub and Rome/Edinburgh as specifically linked external contexts. Borders and jurisdictions changed. Taiwan, full Hong Kong/Macau denominational histories and overseas communities are not covered.

Writer-reviewed research, not independent scholarly approval. Coverage is uneven: Yuan Church of the East, rural Catholics, minority-language histories, women and lay life, wartime institutions and Orthodox jurisdictions need further research. No 2025-2026 current-state certification, population curve or present-day community roster.

Solid interval marks show periods; dashed intervals show approximate or competing date bases, not institutional continuity. Undated gaps remain in the text view. Sorting within partial dates is an editorial convention.

All summaries are original paraphrases with passage locators. No scans, portraits, Bible text or translated extracts are reproduced. Sources express different institutional perspectives; linked versions may depend on the same underlying evidence.
