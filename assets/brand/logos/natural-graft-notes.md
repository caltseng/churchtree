# Main Branch logo studies

Open index.html to compare four natural-color directions at 16, 24, 32 and 96 pixels on light and dark backgrounds. No option is applied to the site.

Palettes vary by study; see the comparison page for exact values. Maximum three colors per mark. Transparent SVG backgrounds; two or three solid colors only; no gradients, external assets or dependencies. Background colors belong only to the comparison page.

- **01 · Woodland**: A direct color study of the leaf-and-graft mark you selected. Warm walnut replaces purple; olive green replaces orange. This isolates how much the palette helps the tree metaphor. Palette: Walnut #986544, Olive #648C45. Files: option-07-natural-original.svg and option-07-natural-original-wordmark.svg.
- **02 · Splice**: A brown rootstock and green shoot meet along a diagonal splice. Two straw-colored ties cross the join. A single leaf makes the new growth explicit. The clearest literal grafting direction. Palette: Bark #9B7555, Leaf #6E9D50, Straw #D6B579. Files: option-08-splice.svg and option-08-splice-wordmark.svg.
- **03 · Side graft**: The brown trunk continues upward while a green shoot joins its side and grows toward the light. A straw collar marks the insertion point. This retains the continuous main trunk of your original favorite. Palette: Bark #9B7555, Leaf #6E9D50, Straw #D6B579. Files: option-09-side-graft.svg and option-09-side-graft-wordmark.svg.
- **04 · New growth**: A green shoot sits inside a visible notch in the brown rootstock. One broad tie secures the union; two leaves balance the silhouette. A softer, more botanical option with a recognizable graft at larger sizes. Palette: Bark #9B7555, Leaf #6E9D50, Straw #D6B579. Files: option-10-cleft.svg and option-10-cleft-wordmark.svg.

Recommended starting point: 02 Splice for the clearest graft metaphor. Each symbol uses a 64 × 64 viewBox and can be used directly as an SVG favicon or image. Provide accessible alt text when the logo is a link. Wordmarks contain live system-font text; outline before a final identity handoff if fixed typography is needed. The standalone symbol contains no font dependency.
