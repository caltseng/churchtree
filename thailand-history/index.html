Main Branch

Church in Thailand

Selected episodes, 1567-2023. Multiple traditions and changing borders; not one national church tree or comprehensive coverage.

Last verified event: 2023. Gaps are not extinction dates.

Scope, gaps and evidence

Present Thailand organizes the selection; Ayutthaya/Siam, northern Lanna/Laos Mission and transborder jurisdictions retain historical meanings.

Sources retrieved 2026-11-24/25; latest selected context 2023, affiliation reference December 2021. Writer self-review, not independent scholarly or Thai-language review. Southern/Isan histories, ordinary worship, other traditions and comparable growth/retention remain incomplete. Conflicting early Bible dates and undated continuities are held, not UI-hidden. Protestant includes independent and charismatic histories; Society groups people, service and state. No copied media or vulnerable contemporary rosters/locations.

Solid interval marks show periods; dashed intervals show approximate or competing date bases, not institutional continuity. Undated gaps remain in the text view. Sorting within partial dates is an editorial convention.

All summaries are original paraphrases with passage locators. No scans, portraits, Bible text or translated extracts are reproduced. Sources express different institutional perspectives; linked versions may depend on the same underlying evidence.
