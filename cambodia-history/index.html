Main Branch

Church in Cambodia

Selected episodes, 1555-2025: Catholic, Protestant, Orthodox and other Christian histories, with language, lay agency, displacement and reconstruction. Introductory, not comprehensive.

Last verified event: 2025-06-28. Gaps are not extinction dates.

Scope, gaps and evidence

Present-day Cambodia is the research boundary, not the whole Khmer Empire. Earlier vicariate territory crossed into Laos and southern Vietnam; Thailand, France and diaspora locations are cross-border context. State names and borders changed.

42 selected milestones from 50 researched rows; eight rows are held outside this public roster, not hidden by filters. Missing research includes wider Pentecostal/Baptist/Adventist/Anglican histories, Khmer scholarship, minority-language communities, Catholic laywomen, primary constitutions, and post-2023 law. Bunong evidence is abstract-only. Salas death years conflict (1976/1977). No comparable national growth series is established: the NIS census-variable page warns its data-file frequencies are not population statistics, and church/WRD/NGO estimates differ. USDOS describes registration and proselytism/inducement restrictions in 2023, not current legal advice. Evidence retrieved November 24, 2026; latest selected event June 28, 2025. Writer self-review is not independent scholarly approval.

Solid interval marks show periods; dashed intervals show approximate or competing date bases, not institutional continuity. Undated gaps remain in the text view. Sorting within partial dates is an editorial convention.

All summaries are original paraphrases with passage locators. No scans, portraits, Bible text or translated extracts are reproduced. Sources express different institutional perspectives; linked versions may depend on the same underlying evidence.

Methods and state context: NIS census-variable warning; USDOS 2023 report, sections I-II. Data-file frequencies, all-age church membership and attendance are not comparable measures.
