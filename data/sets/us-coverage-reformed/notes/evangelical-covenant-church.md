# Evangelical Covenant Church

Audit rank 40, census code 201. The 1885 marker is the Chicago organizing meeting. The Swedish Mission Covenant and American Covenant developed in one transatlantic renewal movement. Evangelical is a broad family placement for this Pietist body.

**Covered within scope.** The [Covenant history](https://covchurch.org/resource/evangelical-covenant-church-history/) records Swedish lay renewal, the early American experiments, education and mission. [North Park yearbooks](https://collections.carli.illinois.edu/digital/collection/npu_covyb) offer primary evidence. The [Covenant Affirmations booklet](https://covchurch.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/covenant_affirmations_booklet.pdf) documents its 1976 publication and 2005 adoption in revised form. The profile covers educational and demographic changes, Willie Jemison and Oakdale, and the vote to ordain women.

**Priority A missing intermediates.** The [Newberry church records guide](https://www.newberry.org/uploads/files/churchguide.pdf) describes the American Mission and Ansgar synods as merger antecedents. The denomination's history describes experiments with these synods. Consult the 1885 yearbook and convention records to determine how the bodies and their congregations entered the new organization. Research the Mission Friends and Swedish Mission Covenant as distinct historical units.

**Priority B missing episodes.** Verify the sequence of English and Swedish names in the yearbooks. Research local mission histories with receiving communities, especially in Alaska, and later dissenting congregations. This covers selected developments through 2005. Global Covenant bodies need separate research.
