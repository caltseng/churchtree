# Evangelical Free Church of America

Unranked census code 203. The 2020 workbook reports 1,602 U.S. congregations and leaves adherents blank. The node begins with the June 1950 union near Minneapolis. Evangelical is the broad family placement. The Covenant and Free Church institutions have distinct histories with overlapping Scandinavian roots.

**Covered within scope.** The [EFCA history](https://www.efca.org/our-history) names both parties. The [1951 yearbook](https://collections.carli.illinois.edu/digital/collection/tiu_efcadir/id/87/) records the early merged body. [Trinity's history](https://www.tiu.edu/history-heritage/) corroborates earlier educational cooperation. Compare the [1950 statement](https://go.efca.org/sites/default/files/resources/docs/2013/04/microsoft-word-1950-sof.pdf) with the [2019 revision](https://www.efca.org/sof/).

**Priority A missing merger partners.** The Swedish Evangelical Free Church and Norwegian-Danish Free Church Association are named predecessor institutions. Both need node-level research and 1950 union records before adding paired merger edges. Verify legal names across phases in the yearbooks.

**Priority B missing episodes.** Research formal ratification and possible non-joining congregations. Expand the history of women and lay leadership and outcomes of diversification and church planting. The scope ends with the 2019 doctrinal revision and a 2020 U.S. census snapshot. International Free Churches are separate historical units.
