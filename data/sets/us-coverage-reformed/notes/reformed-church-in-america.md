# Reformed Church in America

Audit rank 34, census code 371. The 1628 marker is New Amsterdam's first Dutch Reformed congregation and Communion. The 1819 incorporation applies to the General Synod. The RCA name dates to 1867.

**Covered within scope.** [Michaelius's letter](https://readingroo.ms/3/1/6/3161/3161-h/3161-h.htm), the [2021 Church Order](https://www.rca.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/BCO-2021-English.pdf), and the [Van Raalte Institute](https://www.hope.edu/offices/van-raalte-institute/history-institute.html) distinguish settlement, incorporation and immigrant expansion. The [joint account](https://rca.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/Synod2014-ConciseHistory-RCA-CRC.pdf) supports the 1857 edge. Belhar and the post–Vision 2020 departures illustrate later development. The census measure predates those departures.

**Priority A missing intermediates.** The Dutch Reformed Church and Amsterdam classis need institutional records. Use classis correspondence and North American assembly records for the coetus and conferentie disputes and local autonomy. The Genevan Reformed node supplies intellectual context at a greater historical distance.

**Priority B missing episodes.** Trace the eastern True Protestant Dutch Reformed separation and the post–Vision 2020 affiliations from both synod and receiving-body records. More research is needed on women, lay leadership and Indigenous missions. Overseas institutional histories are outside this North American introduction.
