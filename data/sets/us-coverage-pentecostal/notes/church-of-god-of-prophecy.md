# Church of God of Prophecy

Unranked audit gap, code 145. Shared roots do not make this the same present body as Church of God (Cleveland), Church of God in Christ, Church of God (Anderson), or the separate 1993 Charleston body. The early institutional history is represented in the existing Cleveland node. The split edge uses that shared pre-1923 segment and does not settle the competing claims to ecclesial continuity.

**Covered within scope:** division and competing accounts, successor leadership, selected missions, legal naming and continuing teaching. The Tomlinson assembly minutes describe abandoning a constitution; the other branch’s archival finding aid describes his removal and the reorganizing meetings. The March 1952 Tennessee Supreme Court opinion documents the underlying injunction and later dispute; COGOP’s FAQ dates the Bradley County naming decree to 1952, while the Flower Pentecostal Heritage Center catalogs the published case as 1953. The visible naming statement uses the early 1950s pending review of the decree and effective adoption separately. **Missing research:** the precise legal name adoption sequence, post-1943 governance reforms, and parallel evidence on the 1993 departure. The Charleston body is a missing node, and its own account supports the departure date rather than settling its disputed causes.
