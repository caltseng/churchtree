# Church of Our Lord Jesus Christ of the Apostolic Faith, Inc.

Audit rank 48, code 155. The exact corporate name and COOLJC abbreviation distinguish Lawson’s church from Johnson’s Church of The Lord Jesus Christ of the Apostolic Faith, Bible Way, and the Apostolic Faith Mission. The Schomburg finding aid corroborates 1919 in Harlem and distinguishes related bodies; the founding year is an organization marker, not an incorporation claim.

**Covered within scope:** Lawson’s formation, the women’s missionary department, identifiable outgoing branches, and the 1961 and Bonner transitions. **Missing research:** direct archival documentation of Lawson’s relationship to the Pentecostal Assemblies of the World and the mechanisms, disputed causes, and terms of the Johnson and Bible Way separations. Those bodies and any immediate predecessor are missing nodes. The Schomburg collection points to relevant records; materials behind the finding aid were not examined. No edge to a distinct existing Oneness predecessor is supported. Later international jurisdictional details are outside this introductory profile.
