# Vineyard USA

Audit rank 39, code 463. The identity is the U.S. church association; the 1974 chronology is a congregational origin, with 1982 Wimber-era consolidation. Guidepost’s review of the association’s bylaws substantiates development from the Association of Vineyard Churches to the current national governance structure. Classification under Pentecostal reflects charismatic practice and the explorer’s family grouping; the movement describes itself as evangelical and charismatic.

**Covered within scope:** founding network, Wimber’s Anaheim transition, core beliefs and national governance through the 2026 handbook. **Missing nodes:** Calvary Chapel and its historically relevant early California congregations. The published history establishes sending and local disaffiliation; a denomination-wide split edge would overstate them. **Missing research:** original incorporation and name-change records, dates and mechanisms of other significant departures (including Toronto and Anaheim’s later exit), and earlier governance transitions. A global history of Vineyard national bodies and a congregation-by-congregation account are outside this profile.
