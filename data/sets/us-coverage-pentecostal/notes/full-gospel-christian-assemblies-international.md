# Full Gospel Christian Assemblies International

Audit rank 33, FFR. The census label and Illinois ministry use the same distinctive name. Table 1 and Appendix E independently establish the census row and its third-party method, but the census does not publish a roster. The Illinois church history describes a home congregation, overseas activity, and CAIM, without identifying the 722 congregations. The profile attributes those two streams of evidence separately. F23 (Full Gospel Assemblies International, fgai.org) and 232 (Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship) are separate census entries.

**Covered within scope:** founding and lay hosts, Illinois sites, ministry training and media, associated coalition, and the census estimate with its limitations. **Missing research, high priority:** an affiliate register or denominational records linking FFR entries to the Wilson-led ministry, legal formation and polity, and doctrinal texts. CAIM’s relationship to FGCAI needs organizational records before modeling it as a separate institution. Founders’ prior affiliations and any documented mergers or divisions need further research. No institutional edge is supported. International activity is covered only where it explains the U.S. ministry.
