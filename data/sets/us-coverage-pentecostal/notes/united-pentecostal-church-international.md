# United Pentecostal Church International

Unranked audit gap, code 454. The 1945 merger is the start marker. The institutional histories and anniversary account identify both immediate partners and the location; the racial and ethnic affirmation supplies a denominational retrospective on racial divisions before the union. The founding footprint is explicitly North American and comes from the denomination. The displayed 2020 metric is a U.S. congregation count only.

**Missing nodes:** both merger partners (PCI and PAJC), the Pentecostal Assemblies of the World, and intervening bodies in the wider Oneness story. The relationship from the two partners is an institutional merger; no edge compresses either into a link from an unrelated existing denomination. **Missing research:** original merger minutes, the terms and participation of continuing minorities and later PAJC bodies, legal adoption of the current international name, and consequential outgoing separations. Individual national organizations outside the U.S. are out of scope.
