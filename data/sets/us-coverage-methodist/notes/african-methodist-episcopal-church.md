# African Methodist Episcopal Church

Audit rank 17, census group code `003`. Reviewed November 22, 2026. Covers the Philadelphia origins, 1816 denominational organization, selected expansion and leadership milestones, and the 2020 U.S. adherent count. The exact chronology of the St. George’s withdrawal and the development of individual overseas districts require further research.

Covered within stated scope: independent organization from Methodist Episcopal roots, Bethel’s local autonomy, Reconstruction growth, overseas expansion and the opening of the episcopacy to women. Missing research (B): use the court record and St. George’s records to reconcile the chronology of Allen’s retrospective narrative and litigation. No year is assigned to the prayer incident. Missing research (B): use General Conference proceedings and other-party records to assess later divisions and union proposals. The Free African Society and Mother Bethel are contextual institutions, not alternative IDs for the denomination.

## Evidence by claim

- [Our History](https://www.ame-church.com/our-church/our-history/), opening paragraph. Free African Society, racial exclusion at St. George’s, the Bethel dedication, and the struggle for autonomy.
- [Message From the Senior Bishop](https://www.ame-church.com/leadership/message-from-the-senior-bishop/), paragraph beginning ‘While a Senior Bishop’. The denomination was incorporated in 1816.
- [National Register nomination documenting AME origins](https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/3cff29f9-7aab-4d66-b479-af3a1bac8d93), National Register nomination, significance. Mother Bethel site and Allen’s role in founding the denomination.
- [The Life, Experience, and Gospel Labours of the Rt. Rev. Richard Allen](https://classicslaunch.org/works/allen-life-experience-and-gospel-labours/read/), The Life, Experience, and Gospel Labours, St. George’s account. - [Our Beliefs](https://www.ame-church.com/our-church/our-beliefs/), Twenty Five Articles of Religion, especially I, IX, XVI–XVIII. Trinity, justification by faith, baptism, and the Lord’s Supper in the church’s published doctrinal standards.
- [Our History](https://www.ame-church.com/our-church/our-history/), final paragraph. Present connectional structure across multiple continents.
- [Our History](https://www.ame-church.com/our-church/our-history/), geographical spread and Civil War/Reconstruction paragraphs. Reconstruction-era southern expansion and Turner’s promotion of work in Africa.
