# Free Methodist Church–USA

Audit rank 50, census group code `221`. Reviewed November 22, 2026. Covers U.S. organizational origins, selected developments in doctrine and ordination, and the 2020 U.S. adherent count. The start year dates the founding convention of the original Free Methodist Church in the United States.

Covered within stated scope: separation from the Methodist Episcopal Church, 1860 organization and Discipline, and the contested path to equal ordination. Missing research (B): compare Methodist Episcopal Genesee Conference trial minutes and founding convention proceedings to assess the causes and terms of expulsions independently of retrospective Free Methodist accounts. Also compare both parties’ records for later incoming unions or departing groups. Overseas conferences and the history of the worldwide governance structure are out of scope.

## Evidence by claim

- [Historical Heritage & Perspective](https://historical.fmcusa.org/historical-heritage-perspective/), sections on Roberts and the Pekin convention. Reform, denominational controversy and organization by laypeople and clergy at Pekin on August 23, 1860.
- [History of the Free Methodist Church](https://fmcusa.org/history), ‘The Story of B. T. Roberts’. Roberts’s objections to rented pews, slavery and the treatment of the poor, and the formation of the church.
- [The Doctrines and Discipline of the Free Methodist Church](https://historical.fmcusa.org/wp-content/uploads/1860-Book-of-Discipline.pdf), title page and Articles of Religion. Original Discipline adopted August 23, 1860.
- [SCOD Report to the 2003 General Conference](https://scod.fmcusa.org/scod-report-to-the-2003-general-conference/), ‘The Absence of an Article on Entire Sanctification in the Methodist Discipline’. Free Methodists adapted the Methodist Episcopal Discipline and explicitly added an article on entire sanctification.
- [2023 Book of Discipline](https://fmcusa.org/wp-content/uploads/2023-Book-of-Discipline-Interactive-PDF-FINAL.pdf), Foreword and chapter 1, Articles of Religion. Current U.S. general conference and Free Methodist teaching on grace, sanctification and growth.
- [FMC Statement on Women in Ministry](https://scod.fmcusa.org/fmc-statement-on-women-in-ministry/), historical account of 1890, 1891 and 1974. Roberts argued for ordaining women; the General Conference approved equal status for women in ministry in 1974.
- [2020 U.S. Religion Census Group Detail Workbook](https://www.usreligioncensus.org/sites/default/files/2023-06/2020_USRC_Group_Detail.xlsx), 2020 Group by Nation, group code 221, column D (Adherents). 71,634 U.S. adherents in 2020 for Free Methodist Church–USA.
