# African Methodist Episcopal Zion Church

Audit rank 30, census group code `005`. Reviewed November 22, 2026. Covers the New York origins, stages of independence, selected abolitionist history, and the 2020 U.S. adherent count. The timeline uses the 1822 ordinations as the milestone for a fully functioning independent ministry.

Covered within stated scope: local roots, local incorporation, Discipline, ordination and episcopacy as distinct events, abolitionist agency, and the name’s continuity. Missing research (B): compare early conference minutes and charters for the 1820–1822 period and both connections’ voting records for the fate of the 1885 proposal. Later overseas institutional development and regional disputes remain out of scope. Zion and John Street are local congregations, and Philadelphia’s AME is a separate denomination.

## Evidence by claim

- [About The A.M.E. Zion Church](https://amezion.org/about/), Our History, paragraphs on 1796–1801. John Street origins, local incorporation and arrangement for sacramental ministry.
- [About The A.M.E. Zion Church](https://amezion.org/about/), Our History, paragraphs on 1820–1822. Withdrawal, first Discipline, ordinations, and Varick’s election as first bishop.
- [About The A.M.E. Zion Church](https://amezion.org/about/), Our Beliefs. Salvation through Jesus Christ and renewal by divine grace through the Holy Spirit.
- [The Doctrines and Discipline of the African Methodist Episcopal Zion Church in America](https://books.google.com/books?id=SVpPAQAAIAAJ), Articles of Religion and forms for Baptism and the Lord’s Supper. Historical denominational edition documents Methodist doctrines, sacramental forms and church order.
- [History of the A.M.E. Zion Church](https://omeka.hrvh.org/exhibits/show/ame-zion-church-in-new-paltz/history-of-the-a-m-e--zion-chu), Rochester AME Zion Church History. Local work associated with Harriet Tubman, Frederick Douglass and Thomas James, and Christopher Rush’s ministry.
- [About The A.M.E. Zion Church](https://amezion.org/about/), Our History, 1801 charter paragraph. The denomination dates adoption of Zion in the name to 1848 to distinguish it from the Philadelphia AME body.
- [The Star of Zion](https://newspapers.digitalnc.org/lccn/sf88092969/1886-01-22/ed-1/seq-4.pdf), Articles of agreement of the joint commission of the A M E and A M E Zion connections. Joint commission’s 1885 agreement proposed an organic union subject to further action.
- [About The A.M.E. Zion Church](https://amezion.org/about/), Our History, ‘Freedom Church’ paragraph. Current international continuation and abolition and civil-rights heritage.
- [2020 U.S. Religion Census Group Detail Workbook](https://www.usreligioncensus.org/sites/default/files/2023-06/2020_USRC_Group_Detail.xlsx), 2020 Group by Nation, group code 005, column D (Adherents). 304,704 U.S. adherents in 2020 for African Methodist Episcopal Zion Church.
