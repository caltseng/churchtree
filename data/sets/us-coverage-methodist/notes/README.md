# U.S. Methodist and Holiness coverage batch

Introductory profiles for audit ranks 17, 29, 30, 31, 43, and 50. The figures represent U.S. adherents in the 2020 U.S. Religion Census. Institutional status was reviewed November 22, 2026.

## Tradition balance and missing records

- The three historically Black denominations document Black initiative in obtaining church autonomy, including local and conference-level organization, abolitionist activity, and Reconstruction. The Methodist Episcopal Church and its Southern branch already exist. Four sourced edges connect those institutions to the new records.
- The three Holiness profiles distinguish a union, the renaming of an English mission, and a new U.S. denomination after regional disciplinary conflict. They trace selected roles for women and laypeople.
- Priority A missing nodes are the two 1968 merger partners. Their names and roles are visible in the Wesleyan profile. See its notes for the two proposed merger relationships and other candidates. Later absorbed bodies, minority continuations, and the Methodist New Connexion need separate review.
- None of the six audit identities had a standalone node in the prior inventory. The historical names in this set belong to the continuing bodies. The merger partners have separate identities.
