Covered within stated scope: both 1968 partners, adoption versus effective union, holiness teaching, and examples of minorities from each side supported by their own accounts. ## Evidence by claim

- [History in the Wesleyan Discipline](https://discipline.wesleyan.org/wiki/Wesleyan_Church,_The:_History), Wesleyan Methodist and Pilgrim Holiness historical sections. Antislavery Wesleyan Methodist organization and revivalist roots of Pilgrim Holiness.
- [The Formation of The Wesleyan Church](https://discipline.wesleyan.org/wiki/F._The_Formation_of_The_Wesleyan_Church), paragraph 50. Merger rejection in 1959, 1966 approvals, and June 26, 1968 union of the two denominations.
- [Summary of Articles of Religion](https://discipline.wesleyan.org/wiki/Summary_of_Articles_of_Religion), ¶299, summary of Articles of Religion. Scripture, repentance and faith, justification, cleansing, perfecting in love and growth in grace.
- [Our History](https://www.biblemethodist.org/about-us/), Our History, paragraphs on 1967–1968. Account from Wesleyan Methodist churches and ministers who withdrew around the proposed merger.
- [History](https://midwestphc.org/about/history/), paragraphs on 1966–1967. Account from Pilgrim Holiness congregations that organized independently during the merger period.
- [The Wesleyan Advocate and Wesleyan Life](https://www.wesleyan.org/the-wesleyan-advocate-and-wesleyan-life-4902), opening and ‘Wesleyan Life’ paragraphs. Predecessor periodicals joined in 1968 and Wesleyan Life succeeded the merged publication in 2004.
- [Don Hodgins retires after extraordinary service in Canada](https://www.wesleyan.org/don-hodgins-retires-after-extraordinary-service-in-canada-1445), paragraph about Central Canada District. Standard Church of America congregations joined the continuing Wesleyan Church through the 2004 merger.
- [Wesleyan Archives and Historical Library](https://www.wesleyanarchives.com/), collections introduction. Archive collects both merger partners’ records and records of the continuing denomination.
- [2020 U.S. Religion Census Group Detail Workbook](https://www.usreligioncensus.org/sites/default/files/2023-06/2020_USRC_Group_Detail.xlsx), 2020 Group by Nation, group code 467, column D (Adherents). 306,005 U.S. adherents in 2020 for The Wesleyan Church.
