# The Salvation Army

Audit rank 43, census group code `403`. Reviewed November 22, 2026. Covers the international movement’s English origins, adoption of its name, official U.S. expansion and U.S. religious-adherent count. It uses 1865 for the mission’s beginning. National charities, territorial corporations and service recipients are outside the census profile.

Covered within stated scope: founding mission, 1878 renaming and structure, women’s founding agency, U.S. expansion and denominational teaching. Christian Mission is an earlier name of this record. Missing node and relationship (B): the Methodist New Connexion is absent. A founder’s prior affiliation does not establish an institutional split. The U.S. Holiness movement is not used as a parent of the 1865 London organization. Missing research (B): consult Heritage Centre organizational records on early independent U.S. Salvationist bodies and possible offshoots. Territorial incorporation and international disputes are out of scope.

## Evidence by claim

- [History](https://www.salvationarmy.org/history), opening and early timeline entries. The Booths established the East London mission work in 1865 and renamed it in 1878.
- [Report on the records of the Salvation Army](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/nra/lists/GB-2133-SA.htm), introduction to the archive report. The Christian Mission became The Salvation Army in 1878, with a military-style structure.
- [William and Catherine Booth](https://www.salvationarmy.org.au/about-us/our-history/william-and-catherine-booth/), biographies of William and Catherine Booth. Catherine’s co-founding and public preaching, and William’s Methodist background.
- [Handbook of Doctrine](https://www.salvationarmy.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/Handbook%2Bof%2BDoctrine.pdf), Eleven doctrines and chapter 10, ‘People of God’. Wesleyan emphasis on conversion and sanctification, and spiritual practice without prescribed outward rites of baptism and communion.
- [Handbook of Doctrine](https://www.salvationarmy.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/Handbook%2Bof%2BDoctrine.pdf), Foreword and ‘Before you begin reading’. The 1878 foundational deed carried the body’s eleven doctrines.
- [History](https://www.salvationarmy.org/history), introductory paragraph and timeline. Continuing international Christian organization and development of religious and social services.
- [A Global Army](https://www.salvationarmy.org.uk/about-us/international-heritage-centre/virtual-heritage-centre/a-global-army), ‘The United States’. Official pioneer party led by George Scott Railton in 1880.
- [History of The Salvation Army](https://www.salvationarmyusa.org/about-us/our-history/), paragraphs on the first American operations. Early U.S. work and official expansion.
- [2020 U.S. Religion Census Group Detail Workbook](https://www.usreligioncensus.org/sites/default/files/2023-06/2020_USRC_Group_Detail.xlsx), 2020 Group by Nation, group code 403, column D (Adherents). 141,098 U.S. adherents in 2020 for The Salvation Army.
