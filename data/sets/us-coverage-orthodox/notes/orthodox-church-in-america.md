# Orthodox Church in America

Reviewed November 22, 2026. See the field-level citations in the associated profile.

- **Covered within scope (1794 antecedent; 1924–2020 institutional outline).** National Park Service and OCA histories establish the Kodiak mission context; OCA records explain the 1924 Metropolia decision. The Tomos and statute supply the 1970 plot milestone. OCA’s 2016 account of concelebration and the interjurisdictional Assembly listing substantiate communion and participation alongside distinct recognition positions.
- **Missing nodes and relationships, priority A.** The Russian North American mission and American Metropolia lack separate records. A proposed `american-metropolia` record would use the 1924 Detroit council and a 1970 continuation edge to OCA supported by the Tomos and the OCA statute. Any phased model should separately handle Moscow’s continuing Patriarchal parishes, which have a distinct census row, and the history of ROCOR.
- **Missing research, priority A.** A fuller account should compare Moscow, Constantinople and Metropolia archival accounts of 1970 and trace affected parishes. Further research should cover the 1946 Cleveland council, jurisdictional accessions after 1970 and historical Indigenous agency beyond the Kodiak overview. Separate treatment of those episodes would avoid conflating communion, canonical standing and recognition of self-government.
- **Scope.** The U.S. census figures exclude other countries in which OCA operates. No terminal successor applies to the active OCA.
