# U.S. Orthodox coverage batch

This batch covers audit ranks 26, 37, 46, 47 and 49. The 2020 U.S. Religion Census Table 1 provides nationwide counts for each entry. Appendix F describes a congregation-based adherent estimate that includes regular, occasional and marginal participants, including children. Counts describe adherents and congregations in the United States in 2020. They are separate from formal membership rolls and from worldwide church population.

The existing `eastern-orthodox-tradition` record provides communion-level context. `Orthodox` follows its existing family label; `Oriental Orthodox` is a separate family for the three other records. Orthodox–Oriental Orthodox dialogue is outside the scope of these U.S. profiles. The Coptic, Ethiopian and Armenian nodes describe U.S. census aggregates or jurisdictional groupings with historically specific date markers.

The existing Eastern Orthodox node is a tradition-level context record without a governing hierarchy. The prototype edge vocabulary has no communion-affiliation or jurisdiction type. The relevant sees, missions, metropolitanate and earlier jurisdictional phases do not have dedicated endpoints. The per-node notes distinguish those missing nodes and links from intentional scope exclusions. Assembly participation is described in the OCA profile as cooperation between jurisdictions.
