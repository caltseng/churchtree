# Coptic Orthodox Church (United States)

Reviewed November 22, 2026. See the field-level citations in the associated profile.

- **Covered within scope (1968–2020).** St. Mark’s local founding account documents household organizing and 1968 liturgies, which supply the plot marker. The sending of Gabriel Abdelsayed in 1970 and the establishment of the Southern diocese in 1993 describe different stages of institutional development. The liturgy preface documents collaboration between bishops, priests and laypeople on multilingual worship.
- **Unsupported/conflicted.** The parish’s broad claim of North American priority is not adopted here. The profile uses the specifically documented Jersey City services and avoids assigning a single date to a nationwide institution. Egypt’s ancient origins are outside this U.S. plot scope.
- **Missing nodes and relationships, priority A.** The Patriarchate of Alexandria has no dedicated record. A later project can assess individual U.S. dioceses and their formation instruments, beginning with the 1993 Southern diocese. A relationship from a parent see to a collective census grouping would require a way to represent jurisdiction without treating the grouping as a single institution.
- **Missing research, priority B.** Additional diocesan timelines, migration patterns outside the Northeast, and lay and women’s leadership beyond the founding account merit further work. No terminal successor or single merger applies to this aggregate.
