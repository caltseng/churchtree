# Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of America

Reviewed November 22, 2026. See the field-level citations in the associated profile.

- **Covered within scope (1921–2020).** Immigrant parish organization preceded the 1921 clergy–laity congress and the 1922 patriarchal recognition and charter. The latter supplies the plot year. The 1931 charter and 1996 territorial reorganization explain two changes in governance.
- **Missing research, priority A.** The GOARCH charter history and Patsavos’s account describe contested allegiances. Further work should compare parish and Church of Greece archives before giving a fuller account of how particular congregations aligned. Only the plot year is asserted for the first charter because sources describe multiple milestones in 1922.
- **Missing nodes, priority A.** Neither the Ecumenical Patriarchate nor the pre-1996 Greek archdiocese has its own record. A future wider-jurisdiction node would start with 1922 church recognition and could have a 1996 continuation relationship to the U.S. body if the existing node were re-phased. The current node covers both phases as U.S. history. The other 1996 territorial jurisdictions are outside this U.S. batch.
- **Missing research, priority B.** Local organizational changes, the contributions of women’s and lay organizations, and the full charter succession need further inquiry. No terminal successor applies to the active U.S. archdiocese.
