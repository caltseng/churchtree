# Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship International

Reviewed November 22, 2026. This profile covers the U.S. founding and selected developments in the international fellowship. The 1994 date marks public establishment and the New Orleans conference. Full Gospel Christian Assemblies International is a distinct congregation founded in the Chicago area.

## Findings and next research

- **Covered within scope.** The [fellowship](https://www.fullgospelbaptist.org/leadership/) dates establishment to 1994 and the [founder’s recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g682fHXlkUI) puts its 1994 conference at the Superdome. An [affiliate manual](https://storage2.snappages.site/MZSG9T/assets/files/NEW-MEMBER-MANUAL.pdf) puts the initial vision in 1992. Its anniversary material credits Debra B. Morton as co-founder and the affiliate manual documents her women’s ministry leadership.
- **Missing research.** Locate early Council of Bishops proceedings, incorporation or covenant records, and the 1994 program, and identify which congregations joined while retaining other ties. Later profiles confirm Walker’s succession and 2013 announcement. A record of the formal installation is a follow-up. Compare local and national sources before generalizing about ordination and changes in polity.
- **Missing relationships.** Greater Saint Stephen is the founder’s local congregation. Existing nodes do not represent an established parent organization from which the fellowship separated. The [Chicago-area congregation](https://fgcai.org/history/) with a similar name traces its founding to Ron and Barbara Wilson in 1983.
- **Intentionally out of scope.** Country-by-country expansion and church-by-church covenant history. Census code 232 measures U.S. adherents and congregations for 2020.
