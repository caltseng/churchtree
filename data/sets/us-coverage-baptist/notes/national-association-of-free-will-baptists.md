# National Association of Free Will Baptists

Reviewed November 22, 2026. The 1935 date marks organization of the national association. This U.S. profile traces its two antecedent streams and selected national developments. Free Will Baptist is also used for churches outside this association.

## Findings and next research

- **Covered within scope.** The [national history](https://nafwb.org/site/about/history/), [Historical Commission](https://fwbhistory.com/site?p=298) and [2023 Treatise](https://nafwb.org/site/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/2023-NAFWB-Treatise.pdf) distinguish the Palmer and Randall streams, the partial Randall union with Northern Baptists, and both immediate 1935 partners.
- **Missing nodes and relationships.** The Cooperative General Association and General Conference have no exact existing nodes. The Northern Baptist relationship concerns the portion of the Randall movement that joined it, before NAFWB existed. For a future graph expansion, consult the 1910–1911, 1916, 1921 and 1935 minutes. The existing American Baptist Churches USA node cannot stand in for either 1935 partner.
- **Missing research.** Both the official account and the Treatise cite slavery and the Civil War as obstacles to earlier union. Contemporary correspondence and proceedings are needed to assess that causal account, including the roles of Black, lay and women participants. Research later mission and educational institutions and regional bodies that remained independent.
- **Intentionally out of scope.** Country-by-country missions and detailed congregational history. Census code 223 measures U.S. adherents and congregations for 2020.
