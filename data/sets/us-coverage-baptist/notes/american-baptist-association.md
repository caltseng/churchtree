# American Baptist Association

Reviewed November 22, 2026. This profile covers the U.S. association organized in 1924, selected antecedents, the 1950 division and present practice. American Baptist Churches USA is a separate denomination with Northern Baptist roots.

## Findings and next research

- **Covered within scope.** The [current association](https://www.abaptist.org/start-here-page) and [Arkansas institutional history](https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/State-Association-of-Missionary-Baptist-Churches-of-Arkansas-2689/) place national organization in 1924. The 1902 Arkansas association and pre-1924 minutes are antecedents. The [ABA archive](https://baptistville.org/abahistory/) preserves proceedings going back to 1905.
- **Missing nodes and relationships.** Immediate Arkansas and Texas associations and the 1950 departing association are absent as exact records. The distinct American Baptist Churches USA supplies no institutional endpoint. [ABA historical papers](https://www.abahistory.org/_files/ugd/397dfb_da81f478dab24a8fa9d8082dd6ed6bdc.pdf) and the [Mississippi BMA chronology](https://bmams.org/assets/docs/ms_baptists.pdf) document that both fellowships continued after 1950. Compare both sets of minutes before a fuller account of contested representation and mission-finance questions.
- **Missing research.** Inventory all partners at Texarkana, date revisions to the doctrinal statement and mission policy, and document later seminary, publishing, women’s auxiliary and lay ministry work from minutes. The current statements describe published positions today.
- **Intentionally out of scope.** Global mission histories and local biographies. Census code 017, distinct from code 019 (American Baptist Churches USA), measures U.S. adherents and congregations for 2020.
