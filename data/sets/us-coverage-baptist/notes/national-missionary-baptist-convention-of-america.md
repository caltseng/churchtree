# National Missionary Baptist Convention of America

Reviewed November 22, 2026. This U.S. profile covers the convention organized in Dallas in 1988, its early education work and selected evidence of its continuation. It is distinct from National Baptist Convention of America (NBCA), National Baptist Convention, USA, Inc. (NBCUSA), and Progressive National Baptist Convention (PNBC).

## Findings and next research

- **Covered within scope.** The [convention](https://www.nmbca.org/who-we-are) dates its Dallas organization to 1988. The [parent annuals finding aid](https://sbhla.org/digital-resources/national-baptist-america-annuals/) and [contemporary reporting](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/local/1988/12/03/black-baptist-group-splits-over-control-of-education/48cb198e-6e81-4556-9266-ae1ae906e82c/) agree on separation from NBCA over publishing. The report captures opposing governance aims. The archived Afro-American item documents plans for the Chicago session.
- **Unsupported or conflicted.** A [Louisville Seminary guide](https://lpts.libguides.com/denominations/nmbca) names NBCUSA as the parent. The 1988 reporting explicitly distinguishes the two, and the archive places the split within NBCA. Some participants framed the separation as a restoration. The record dates the present organization to the Dallas meeting.
- **Missing research, high priority.** Locate Dallas minutes and early NMBCA constitutions or confessions, verify the Chicago session from proceedings, and document the reported Institutional Missionary Baptist Conference of America departure with records from both parties. The current mission text establishes ministry priorities. Early doctrine and congregational variations remain open. Women's Auxiliary forms attest to its existence. Its founding and early leadership need records.
- **Intentionally out of scope.** Foreign mission history and local congregational histories.
- **Metrics.** Code 302 is the 2020 national adherent and congregation count. The organization's separately advertised membership has different scope or reference period and supplies no trend comparison. Codes 301, 300 and 364 are separate active convention identities.
