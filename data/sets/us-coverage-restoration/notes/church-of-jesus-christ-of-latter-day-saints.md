# The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

Reviewed November 22, 2026. Audit rank 4, code 151. The node covers the church organized in New York in 1830 and the branch that followed Brigham Young after 1844. The historical narrative is U.S.-centered, with selected milestones through 1978 and a 2020 demographic snapshot.
