## Missing-neighborhood priorities

A = missing ancestry/merger partners that distort a represented path;
B = major omission within intended country/tradition/episode coverage;
C = optional breadth or narrower additional coverage.

No unresolved A path is published: no institutional lineage edges are imported.
This does **not** make B gaps optional. Catholic jurisdictions and Methodist
agencies are preserved as distinct units in entities.md; they are not collapsed
to make a connected national graph. The public route contains episode records,
not a completed national denomination catalog. A later institutional import
would elevate missing primary constitutions/intermediates to A.

## Exact disposition distinctions

**Researched but not displayed:** KH03, KH07, KH10, KH11, KH26, KH44,
KH46, KH48. Reasons are in chronology and canonical `held`. These are not
present-but-UI-hidden: there are no corresponding public milestone records.
Facts retained in other event caveats (for example KH26) are not duplicate nodes.

**Present but UI-hidden:** a selected dated event can be outside the current
search/date/lane window or in a cluster. Its stable ID remains in approved
public data and generated HTML, and Text view/reset/deep link can reach it.
This engineering condition does not describe the eight held rows.

**Never adequately researched:** the missing questions above. They have no
invented dated placeholder record. "Not applicable" applies only to corporate
merger/constitution categories for people, texts, periods and countries, which
are not corporations. It never excuses an unexamined church.

## Review limitations and rights

The writer checked all admitted locators against the retrieved passages and
restricted abstract-only sources to their abstract claims. The PDF of Wong was
read only to the extent recorded in sources.md; underlying CMA archives,
inaccessible monographs and the full Scheer article are not claimed as read.
English dominates the evidence. Khmer-language LDS official material and Italian
Vatican reporting broaden language access but do not replace Khmer scholarship.

No copied media, Bible/lyric extracts, modern ethnographic identities, children's
data, medical details, registration rosters or vulnerable current meeting sites
are included. A separate dependent audit must retain its independent question/
answer baseline before repository comparison and repair the actual evidence,
not just count fields or mechanically approve this packet.
