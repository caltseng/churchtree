Evidence and citation locators: [sources](sources.md). Geography is present
Cambodia unless explicitly external. Dates are source-supported text, not
renderer coordinates. A span marked *period* is duration; *window* is uncertain
placement, not continuous activity. Retrospective publication dates are not
event dates. Foundation rows writer-reviewed 2026-11-24; audit repairs and
appended rows writer-reviewed 2026-11-25, not independently approved.

`Selected` maps one-to-one to public `research` crosswalks. `Held` means researched
but not in the public milestone roster, not filtered/hidden by UI. Exact public
slugs are in `cambodia-history/data.cjs`; Handoff documents verification.
