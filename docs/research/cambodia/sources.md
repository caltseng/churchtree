## Leads not admitted as read evidence

S02 cites J. Planet, *Histoire de la Mission du Cambodge* (1929), François
Ponchaud, *La cathédrale de la rizière* (1990), and early Dominican studies;
S04 gives named CMA annual reports and Clymer's *Troubled Relations*. These
are archival/scholarly follow-up routes, not sources whose unseen pages this
writer claims to have checked. Full Scheer article and authenticated Khmer
constitutional text remain priority acquisitions. Search extracts were discovery
only. No generated encyclopedia/AI summary is admitted as historical evidence.
