## Review performed and limitations

- Read the listed primary/translated passages and selected scholarly PDFs,
  Chinese museum/historical entries and biographies; compared the treaty
  signature independently of its secondary interpretation.
- Kept discovery-only abstracts, failed retrievals and poorly extracted Chinese
  scholarship separate from passage-approved evidence. An indexed Chinese
  parallel announcement is not described as fully read.
- Checked the Tang arrival/testimony distinction, Macao date qualification,
  Liang age conflict, Jesus Family staged formation, Nee co-worker date,
  Orthodox decision/implementation, council/union identities, communion/civil
  recognition and demographic denominators.
- Rechecked new source errors: Richard's 1915 retirement/death header,
  Guo/Wang's inconsistent hymn arithmetic, Sung's Dora Yu chronology,
  Pollard's meeting sequence and Wang's imprisonment-duration wording.
  Repairing a limited claim does not validate the rest of its source.
- Reviewed historical units and relationship direction/type substantively,
  including rejected edges. No institutional bridges are inferred from sequence;
  no complete merger genealogy, single national church or universal termination
  is claimed.
- Source balance remains uneven: early medieval modern scholarship and Chinese
  social history are access-limited, several Protestant accounts share a
  confessional platform, modern Catholic primary documents principally express
  Rome's perspective, and Orthodox history is not independently corroborated.
  The packet remains useful as a bounded foundation, not a release-approved
  comprehensive country dataset. Its pre-comparison baseline, post-comparison
  source-access work, integrated repairs and residual priorities are retained
  in the applied audit rather than presented as completed gap elimination.

Mechanical validation and publication-isolation checks are reported in the PR,
not confused with scholarly review. Later import must resolve the applicable
priority-A items and the claim-specific release gates in the
[handoff](handoff.md).
