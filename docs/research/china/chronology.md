# Annotated chronology

Research rows, **not importable production records**. P/S/Q have the meanings
defined in the [packet entry](README.md#how-to-read-evidence-status).
Each row includes event/date basis, testimony date and a limiting annotation.
Year-only dates must not acquire artificial month/day precision in conversion.
Entity IDs resolve in the [entity register](entities.md); C IDs resolve in
[sources](sources.md). Geography refers to the historical setting, not a
constant national polygon. Relative dates remain relative.

## Demographic annotations, not a growth curve

These are rounded comparisons reproduced and assessed by **Pew (2023)**, not
new estimates produced for this packet. Citation [C26](sources.md#c26) contains
the precise passage groups; [C27](sources.md#c27) describes the survey limits.

| ID | Reference period; publisher/method | Measure / universe and reported magnitude | Interpretation constraint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D01 | 2018; CGSS, Renmin University; analyzed by Pew | Christian self-identification, adults, approximately 2% / 20 million | Adult survey-derived affiliation, not all ages or all private believers. Survey geography does not cover every mainland region. |
| D02 | 2018; CFPS, Peking University; analyzed by Pew | Adults reporting belief in Jesus and/or Tianzhu, approximately 7% / 81 million | Belief question allows a different and potentially overlapping religious universe; not an alternate estimate of D01's identical measure. |
| D03 | 2010 versus 2018; CGSS comparison by Pew | Change in measured Christian affiliation not statistically significant | Does not demonstrate either no real change or exponential growth; 2021 narrower sampling is not directly comparable. |
| D04 | 2018; government figures reported by Pew | Approximately 38 million Protestants | Administrative/official estimate, not the same method or age universe as D01. Do not equate recognized Protestantism with every Christian. |
| D05 | 2020; Asia Harvest advocacy estimate reported by Pew | 109.7 million all-age Protestants | Underlying method/rosters not independently inspected; Pew discusses potential double-counting/overestimation. Not independent validation of a national total. |
| D06 | 2010-2018 official; 2020 HSSC/Asia Harvest, reported by Pew | Catholic figures differ: official approximately 6 million; HSSC approximately 10 million in 2020; Asia Harvest approximately 20 million in 2020 | Definitions, registered/unregistered scope and estimation differ. Do not average, sum, or splice into one continuous series. These are not measurements of communion status. |

No denominational membership, survey self-identification, belief response or
reported administrative total is converted into an inferred conversion count.
