# Entities, aliases and relationship evidence

These are research identities, not production denomination nodes. Separate
country-history actors from institutions, movements, documents and context.
An entity's first attestation is not necessarily its formation. Chinese forms
are limited to inspected source material; other forms await local-language
authority control. Do not silently generate characters from a romanized name.
