## Excluded leads, errors and dependence

- LiCAS.news 2019 concise history was read as an institutional cross-check,
  **not** independent corroboration. Its Laneau dates conflict internally;
  its 379,975 / 69 million / 0.46% statement is arithmetically inconsistent.
  Neither its precision nor implied growth enters the public data.
- WCRC's 2025 *Protestant Church and Thailand* was read. It mixes a 1868
  congregation with a 1878 first-church claim and has unsourced current
  totals. It is not admitted for those claims; C06 is the bounded 1828 source.
- S11's general 1881 Christian-origin sentence conflicts with earlier evidence;
  S17's state-religion/2% statements conflict with the more explicitly dated
  S15. Those errors are not silently propagated.
- S17 gives incompatible 2025 membership totals and 1925/1930 first-ethnic-Thai
  baptism dates; none is adopted. H35's 1987 hospital closure remains explicitly
  provisional pending its cited older encyclopedia entry (footnote 38).
  [Hat Yai Focus's retrospective](https://www.hatyaifocus.com/forums-detail/1300/),
  2019-07-20, opening hospital-history paragraphs read on 2026-11-25, supports
  a former hospital/1954 opening, but does not independently establish 1987
  or the staffing explanation; its modern site/address/pictures are not copied.
- A newly indexed Thai Chiang Mai Theological Journal pioneer article could
  not be fetched (DNS failure). Thai government religious-survey catalog
  returned403; search metadata suggests an age 13+ universe, not the RAD
  series. Anglican parish history URLs returned404; current welcome/about
  pages cannot prove nineteenth-century dates. These are **leads**, not
  evidence for unseen histories.
- Gutenberg memoir returned503; Internet Archive OCR supplied the lawful
  accessible edition. Some Thai Missions PDFs returned403; author-hosted
  Swanson scans supplied the inspected texts. OCR was used cautiously;
  uncertain names/words do not justify new precision.
- Payap archives and sources cited inside Swanson/Hayami are research leads,
  not archival documents read by this writer. S03/S04/S05 are distinct works
  by one historian; S01/S12, S08-S10, and S03/S07 each share institutional
  contexts. No spurious independent witness count is claimed.
